Why choose Screen Privacy Blur

It focuses on fast browser-based privacy controls instead of full video editing, desktop capture suites, or post-production workflows. Create an overlay, place it where you need it, and present.

Best fit

Use it when you want to keep presenting in Chrome while hiding predictable sensitive areas from calls and recordings. It is especially useful for live demos, support sessions, and internal walkthroughs.

  • Chrome-first
  • Local blur
  • Quick setup
  • Manual and Pro workflows

How to prepare before sharing

When setting up Safe screen share alternative (Safe Presentation Alternative | Screen Privacy Blur), Screen Privacy Blur runs 100% locally in your Chromium browser so private records, credentials, and financial balances remain hidden or replaced with realistic demo data before any frame is shared.

  1. Open the browser tab or desktop context you plan to present.
  2. Place blur overlays over the private fields that can appear during the walkthrough.
  3. Review the visible area, then start the call or recording.

Frequently asked questions

How does Screen Privacy Blur compare to Safe Screen Share extensions?

Screen Privacy Blur runs locally in a Manifest V3 Chrome Side Panel with free manual element and area blur, per-website saved layouts, and optional Pro masks and realistic synthetic data replacement for live demos.

How do Manual Blur, Smart Auto-Blur, and Auto-Replace work for Safe screen share alternative?

For Safe screen share alternative (Alternative), Manual Blur is free forever and lets you click exact DOM elements or draw custom frosted-glass regions (Chrome-first, Local blur, Quick setup, Manual and Pro workflows). Smart Auto-Blur Pro masks detected emails, phones, and API keys locally, while Auto-Replace Pro swaps sensitive text with realistic demo data.

Does any page data leave my browser during Alternative sessions?

No. When using Screen Privacy Blur for Safe screen share alternative, all pattern matching and DOM replacement run 100% locally inside the Manifest V3 Chrome extension. Open Chrome DevTools → Network while toggling Alternative protection to verify zero page content is uploaded.

Can I save my Alternative layout for future calls and recordings?

Yes. Once you configure Safe screen share alternative rules (Chrome-first, Local blur, Quick setup, Manual and Pro workflows) on a domain, Screen Privacy Blur saves that layout locally so your protection is ready automatically before your next Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Loom, or OBS session.
